Yohta Chibi Mohawk - layered TinyPaw character

Import: Settings > Character > Import folder, then choose this folder (the one containing character.json). If downloaded as a ZIP, extract first.

Layers: sit/head.png (ears, mohawk and static eyes included), sit/body.png (paws included), sit/tail.png. Every layer is aligned on the same 1290 x 1219 transparent canvas.

The visible artwork was cut from your approved image. Small hidden neck and tail-root overlaps were reconstructed with the image-generation tool. The original is included as approved-original.png.

Head and tail have gentle motion presets. This pack does not add music support to the app; music response depends on the installed TinyPaw build and its settings. Eye tracking and separate ear/hair motion are not configured.
